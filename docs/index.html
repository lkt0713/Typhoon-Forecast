About

How to read this site

AI and physics-based ensemble forecasts for Western Pacific tropical cyclones, redrawn in one style every 10–15 minutes beside the official forecasts from JTWC, JMA (Japan) and CWA (Taiwan). One model menu switches every chart to the same model.

  • Top of a storm page — current wind, pressure and movement, Taiwan and Japan risk, what changed since the last run, and an interactive map with a time slider.
  • Summary — satellite loop, the environment (ocean heat, wind shear, steering flow), official forecasts, and Taiwan landfall, city and county probabilities.
  • Track — model comparison, ensemble maps, animation and run-to-run trend.
  • Strike — wind radii, strike probability by area and period, and the risk at your own location.
  • Intensity — intensity forecast and a bias-corrected line.
  • Verification — track, along-/cross-track and intensity errors for every model.
  • Archive — every past storm with its real track, report card, downloads and season-to-date scores.

⚠️Notice

Not for operational use. For official warnings, follow your national meteorological agency.

Probabilities on this site are the share of ensemble members that forecast an outcome — a measure of how much the models agree, not an official forecast probability. A deterministic run (AIFS-single, ECMWF HRES, AIGFS, GFS) is a single possible scenario.

🔔Alerts

New systems, naming, intensity-category changes, forecast shifts of 150 km or more and Taiwan landfall chance changes of 10 points or more, as soon as an update notices them.
