Along-track error: measured along the storm's real direction of motion, is the forecast position ahead of (+) or behind (−) the real storm? A steady + means the model moves the storm too fast, − too slow. The small |e| is the average size ignoring the sign. Only counted while the storm moves at least 5 km/h; when it stalls or loops its direction is unreliable.

Intensity error: difference between the forecast maximum wind and the JTWC maximum wind (kt, average size ignoring the sign). The small "bias" is the signed average: + = too strong, − = too weak. Ensemble means smooth out the members and usually run weak, and coarser models often underestimate strong typhoons. Wind speeds were only archived from late October 2026 on, so older forecasts have no intensity to compare.
